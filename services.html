INTEGRATION

Direct engineering partnership to plug axiom-warp-jdbc, axiom-warp-reactive, and custom parsers into performance-critical services without framework bloat. Typically 2–6 weeks for module integration, or an ongoing retainer for full pipeline design; final scope confirmed after an initial codebase walkthrough.

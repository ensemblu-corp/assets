FAQ

Licensing questions, answered.

Is Axiom 3 open source?

The source is public, and every module ships with a sources JAR on Maven Central. Axiom is not under an OSI open-source license, though. It's published under the Limited Commercial License: free for evaluation, testing, education and non-commercial development, with a paid annual agreement required for production or commercial use.

What counts as commercial use?

Any use in a production environment, or for any purpose that generates revenue or supports a business activity. If you're unsure whether your case qualifies, ask; we'd rather answer up front.

Do I need a license to try it at work?

Evaluation and testing are free, including prototypes and proofs of concept. The license is needed once Axiom runs in production or supports a business activity.

Does one license cover all modules?

Yes. The commercial agreement covers the Axiom 3 stack: the five core modules and the five native drivers.

What about Axiom 2.x?

The 2.0.0 artifacts stay on Maven Central under the same evaluation terms, but commercial agreements and engineering work target Axiom 3. If you're on 2.x, the migration guide covers the move to 3.0, and migration help is available as a service.

Can I sponsor the project instead?

Sponsorship through GitHub Sponsors funds independent development and is very welcome. It doesn't replace a commercial license for production use.
